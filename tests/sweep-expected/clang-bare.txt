# Three of the five are the unoptimized-build case gcc-bare also carries. The other two are
# clang's alone and persist under every profile: see clang-bare-optimized.txt.

# fortify-level-ge-2-subobject is not listed. Its positive control is silent here, and the
# cause is measured: glibc 2.39's strcpy wrapper asks __builtin_object_size(__dest, 1)
# inside an always-inline function, where clang 18.1.3 answers 16, the whole object, and
# gcc 13.3.0 answers 8. The probe prints that answer as wrap1, and the gating rule
# subobject-bound-lost-in-wrapper reads it and publishes unsupported, an earned limitation
# this file does not list. The gap is the pairing of this compiler with that wrapper form,
# not glibc's alone: glibc 2.43 with clang 22.1.8 on Fedora 44 aborts. See the project
# ledger.
strict-flex-arrays indeterminate __builtin_object_size answers -1 at every level without optimization, so no bound is known and a flag that narrows one cannot be read
no-strict-overflow indeterminate clang keeps the overflow guard in an unoptimized build whether or not the flag was passed, so the arms cannot be separated
no-delete-null-pointer-checks probe-invalid the negative control shows the observable too, so on clang the two arms cannot be told apart and the detector is not demonstrated to work here
no-strict-aliasing indeterminate the store is observed whether or not the flag was passed at -O0, so the arms cannot be separated
